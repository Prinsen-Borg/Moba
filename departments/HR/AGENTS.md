## Extra rules for HR (stricter than the company default)

- **No personal data in this repository, ever.** That covers employee names linked to performance, salaries, absence, health, reviews and complaints.
- An assistant may help write *templates*, *policies* and *anonymised* analyses. Individual cases stay in the HR system.
- Texts aimed at employees are written in Dutch unless the audience is international.
