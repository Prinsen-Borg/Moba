# AGENTS.md: IT & Digital Transformation

Department layer on top of the company rules in `/AGENTS.md`. Follow both. The top-level file wins on security, data and brand.

## Who we are

- **IT & Digital Transformation** runs Moba's business applications, infrastructure, integrations, data, service desk and IT projects for a company active in more than 100 countries.
- The team has about 30 internal staff and about 50 including contractors and outsourced partners. It is led by the Director IT & Digital Transformation.
- Teams: business IT consultants and application management · infrastructure (network, servers, datacentre) · service desk · integrations · data · project pool · enterprise architecture.

## What matters now (Q4 2026)

1. **Closer to the business.** The IT team itself named better contact with the business as its top priority. Write for business readers: outcomes first, jargon explained.
2. **Security foundations.** Moba has no formal security framework yet. Every proposal should state its security impact.
3. **Deliberate cloud choices.** Moba's cloud platform is Azure. Weigh on-premise against cloud explicitly in every infrastructure decision.
4. **AI with structure.** The aim is to move from ad-hoc AI use to a governed approach, through this repository, Copilot and Claude.

## How we work

- Portfolio files live in `portfolio/`. One initiative is one row in `initiatives.md`, and the status is updated monthly.
- Use the status scale **On track · At risk · Off track · Not started · Decision needed**.
- Never put budgets, contract values or supplier pricing in this folder; link to the SharePoint location instead.
- Pages and reports follow `DESIGN.md` and the `moba-web-page` skill.
