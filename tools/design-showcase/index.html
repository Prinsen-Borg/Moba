Colour

Navy for words, amber for action

A calm grey canvas carries deep navy type. Amber is kept for the one thing you want people to click, and bronze lifts a single word in a headline.

Typography

A warm serif for headlines, a friendly sans for everything else

IBM Plex Serif gives headlines an established, trustworthy voice. Ubuntu keeps the interface and body copy open and technical. On Windows laptops without these fonts, Georgia and Segoe UI take over.

Spacing

Ten steps from 8 to 100 pixels

Use only these values for gaps and padding. Sections breathe with 64–100px between them, and cards use 24–32px inside.

Components

Pills, flat cards and the egg

Buttons are pills with a small navy arrow. Cards are white, rounded and never shadowed. A small egg marks list items and links.

Buttons

One primary per screen. Button text stays navy, because white on amber fails contrast.

Imagery

One big rounded corner

Hero images get a 75px radius on the top-left corner only. An amber block can sit partly behind the image as an accent. Use real photography of farms, factories and machines rather than stock illustrations.

Rules of thumb

Do and don't

✓Navy on light grey as the default for every page.
✓One bronze word per headline, on the idea that matters most.
✓Real numbers, such as "45,000 eggs/hour", instead of adjectives.
✓Eyebrow, serif heading, short lead at the start of each section.
✕No shadows on cards, and no purple or blue "tech" gradients.
✕No amber text and no large amber areas.
✕No all-caps headlines and no emoji as section markers.
✕No recoloured logo. Ask Marketing for the official variants.
